# Billing

The Billing page is your financial dashboard. It shows your account balance, spending trends, and provides multiple ways to top up.

## Account Balance

When you open the **Billing** page, you'll see four summary cards at the top:

| Card | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Remaining Balance** | Your current account balance in USD |
| **Used Balance** | Total amount spent to date in USD |
| **Requests (24h)** | Number of API requests made in the last 24 hours |
| **Tokens (24h)** | Total tokens consumed in the last 24 hours |

## Topping Up

### Stripe Payment

1. Click the **Top Up** button on the Billing page
2. Choose a preset amount: $10, $20, $50, $100, $200, or $500
3. Or enter a custom amount (subject to minimum and maximum limits)
4. You'll be redirected to Stripe to complete the payment
5. After a successful payment, refresh the page to see your updated balance

### Redemption Code

If you have a redemption code (gift card or promotional code):

1. Click the **Top Up** button
2. Switch to the **Redemption Code** tab
3. Enter your code and click **Redeem**
4. The corresponding amount will be added to your balance immediately

## Affiliate Program

Earn credits by referring others to TokenGO:

### Get Your Referral Link

1. On the Billing page, find the **Affiliate Program** section
2. Copy your unique referral link
3. Share it with others — when they sign up using your link, they become your referral

### Track Referrals

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Referrals** | Number of people who signed up using your link |
| **Pending Credits** | Referral credits waiting to be transferred to your balance |
| **Total Earned** | Cumulative credits earned from referrals |

### Transfer Credits

Click **Transfer to Balance** to move pending referral credits into your main account balance. This is a manual action — credits are not transferred automatically.

## Performance Metrics

The Billing page also displays 24-hour performance metrics:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Avg TPM** | Average tokens per minute over the last 24 hours |
| **Avg RPM** | Average requests per minute over the last 24 hours |
| **Peak TPM** | Highest tokens per minute recorded in the last 24 hours |
| **Avg Tokens/Request** | Average number of tokens consumed per request |

## Usage Trends

The usage trend chart shows your consumption over the last 24 hours, broken down by hour. You can switch between three views:

- **Quota** — Spending in USD per hour
- **Tokens** — Token consumption per hour
- **Requests** — Number of requests per hour

Below the chart, the **Top Models by Quota** section ranks your most-used models by cost. Expand any model to see a detailed breakdown.

## Billing Calculation

TokenGO charges based on actual token consumption:

```
Cost = (Prompt Tokens / 1,000,000 x Input Price) + (Completion Tokens / 1,000,000 x Output Price)
```

If your API key is assigned to a group with a ratio other than 1.0, the final cost is multiplied by the group ratio:

```
Final Cost = Cost x Group Ratio
```

Some models also support **cache pricing** — cached input tokens are billed at a lower rate. Check the [Model Hub](/docs/user-guide/model-hub) for each model's input, output, and cache prices.
